# Simplify the journey around the shelf

## Goal
Make expiry tracking and routine conflict checks the clearest reason to use My Beauty Shelf, while keeping Shelfie available as a standalone feature without promoting it during setup.

## What will change
- Remove the Shelfie step, undertone prompt, Shelfie destination, and related copy from onboarding.
- Replace the landing-page “Shelfie skin score” feature with a shelf-focused benefit about scanning products and checking expiry/conflicts.
- Change the main landing action to **Build my beauty shelf** and make the first-time journey product-first.
- Stop forcing newly signed-in customers into the skin questionnaire.
- Preserve the optional skin profile for later personalization through **My skin**; keep the existing standalone Shelfie tab and page.

## Reviewed journey
The current flow sends the landing action to a five-step skin profile, and the home page forces incomplete profiles back there. Adding a product requires an account. The revised journey therefore uses a short account handoff, remembers that the customer wants to add a product, and continues directly to the product camera after sign-in.
